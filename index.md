# Privacy Policy for Snapora

**Effective date: August 27, 2026**

Snapora is developed and published by **Ghawi Apps**.

Snapora is designed to organize screenshots directly on your Android device. We value privacy and have designed the app so that your screenshots and folder organization remain on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

## Information Snapora Accesses

Snapora may request access to:

- Photos and images on your device, including screenshots, so the app can detect, organize, display, move, hide, restore, and manage screenshots.
- Permission to display over other apps so Snapora can show folder shortcuts after a screenshot is taken.
- Android system permissions required for reliable screenshot detection and background operation.

These permissions are used only to provide Snapora's core functionality.

## Screenshots and Media

Snapora processes screenshots and other media locally on your device.

Snapora does **not** upload your screenshots, images, folder contents, or media to Ghawi Apps servers.

Depending on your settings, organized screenshots may:

- remain visible in your Android Gallery and other media applications; or
- be stored as app-only media so they are available through Snapora but hidden from normal Gallery and media pickers.

You control these options inside the app.

## Accounts

Snapora does not require you to create a Snapora account.

We do not operate a cloud account system for storing your screenshots or folders.

## Personal Data

Ghawi Apps does not collect or sell the contents of your screenshots.

Snapora does not sell personal information to advertisers or other third parties.

## Payments and Subscriptions

Snapora may offer optional paid subscriptions through **Google Play Billing**.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Ghawi Apps does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Snapora may receive information from Google Play indicating whether a purchase or subscription is active so that paid features can be enabled.

For information about Google's handling of payment and account information, please refer to Google's own privacy policies.

## Advertising

Snapora does not currently display third-party advertisements.

## Data Storage and Retention

Folder settings, preferences, and app configuration are stored locally on your device.

Screenshots and organized media remain on your device until you delete them.

Some recovery information may also be stored locally on your device so that Snapora folders can be restored after reinstalling the app.

Uninstalling Snapora may remove app-specific settings. Media stored in shared device storage may remain on the device until you delete it.

## Sharing

Snapora does not automatically share your screenshots with other people or services.

If you choose to make a folder visible to Android Gallery or use Android's sharing features, you control which media is shared with other applications.

## Security

Snapora is designed to minimize external data transfer by processing screenshot organization locally on the device.

No method of electronic storage or software security can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we limit access and processing to what is necessary for the app's functionality.

## Children's Privacy

Snapora is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Snapora's features, permissions, or services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
